Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ultimate Organizer ("the app") is a personal and family task organizer. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and to send group invitations.
  • Your name, which is shown to other members of any group you join.
  • The tasks and ideas you create, including their titles, dates, and recurrence settings.
  • The groups you create or join and their membership.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any analytics or tracking information about how you use the app.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to operate the app: to authenticate you, to display your tasks and ideas, to let group members assign tasks to one another, and to show shared group ideas to group members. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How your information is stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access is restricted so that you can only see your own tasks and the shared content of groups you belong to. You can read Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

Sharing within groups

If you join a group, other members of that group can see your name, tasks they assign to you, and any ideas posted to that group's shared board. Personal tasks and ideas that are not assigned to a group remain private to you.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Setup screen in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, your tasks, your ideas, and any groups you own, along with your login credentials. This action cannot be undone.

Children

The app is intended for use by families and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
